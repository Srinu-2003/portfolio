About

I'm finishing an M.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering at JNTU Hyderabad, after graduating as the B.Tech (CSE) batch topper from Mahatma Gandhi University with a CGPA of 8.73/10. My work sits at the intersection of machine learning and deep learning: turning messy, real-world data into models that hold up outside the notebook.

Across two data science internships and four academic projects, I've built classifiers for astronomical spectra, semiconductor measurements, earthquake-damaged roads, and satellite imagery — plus a complete forensic face-sketch recognition platform, from model to database to a working web interface. I care as much about the unglamorous parts — cleaning data, validating models honestly, writing code someone else could pick up — as I do about the accuracy numbers.

I'm looking for a full-time Data Scientist role where I can keep closing the gap between a promising model and a decision someone can actually rely on.
